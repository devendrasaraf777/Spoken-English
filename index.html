Privacy notice

We keep your email address, your name, your mobile number if you give it, your practice settings, and a count of the tutor replies you use. We do not keep recordings of your voice.

What you say is turned into text by your browser's speech service (Google's, in Chrome). The text is sent to Anthropic's Claude to prepare the tutor's reply.

Payments are handled by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details.

You can delete your account and its data at any time from your profile.
